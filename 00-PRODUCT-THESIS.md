# Product thesis

Most travel products answer one of four questions: where to go, whether you can enter, whether you are safe, or what to book. Culture Context is built around a different job:

> Tell me what I do not know I need to know about being here.

The product combines sourced legal/regulatory context, cultural expectations, local impact, and change awareness against a traveler profile. The differentiator is not an LLM. It is the normalized context graph and the provenance/change history underneath the interface.
